I've been writing here for a year now.

It started as an experiment. I gave myself one rule: publish something every week, no matter how small. Not a masterpiece. Just *something*.

Most of what I published isn't very good. Some of it I cringe at now. But here's what happened: after three months, I stopped being afraid of the publish button. After six months, I started to have ideas I wanted to write about. After a year, I actually know what I think about certain things — because writing forced me to figure it out.

---

The craft stuff comes with time. Voice, structure, clarity — all of that improves if you do it enough. But the hardest part is never the craft. It's the showing up.

Same with code. Same with design. Same with anything worth doing.

Show up. Do the work. The rest follows.
