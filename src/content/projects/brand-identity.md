## Brief

A small creative studio needed a cohesive visual identity — something that felt contemporary without chasing trends, and professional without being corporate.

## Process

Started with typography. The choice of typeface set the tone for everything else — a geometric sans-serif with good weight range and strong numerals (important for a studio that does a lot of data work).

Colour came next: a near-black background, one strong accent, and a small set of neutral greys. Enough to create hierarchy, not enough to become chaotic.

## Deliverables

- SVG logo in 4 variants (full, mark, horizontal, mono)
- Typography guide with scale and usage rules
- Colour system with light and dark mode values
- Figma component library with buttons, cards, and navigation patterns
- PDF brand guidelines document

## Reflection

Brand work is humbling. You spend weeks on something that looks deceptively simple. That simplicity is the whole point — and it's much harder to achieve than complexity.
